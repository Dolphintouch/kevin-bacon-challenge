🇳🇱 NETHERLANDS | 7 NOVEMBER 2026

Ingrid's Adventure Begins

Ingrid, known on Geocaching as Secretbox, kindly volunteered to help our little travellers explore the world.

On 22 October 2026, a special parcel left Australia for the Netherlands. Ingrid confirmed receiving it on 7 November!

"I picked up the parcel and it is amazing. Love what you did."

Ingrid enjoys geocaching by bicycle in the Netherlands and hopes to go geocaching with her family during a Christmas visit to South Carolina, USA.

We discussed starting the travel bug's journey in the Netherlands, with the possibility of further adventures overseas.

The parcel's arrival is confirmed. Its placement in a geocache has not yet been confirmed.

🇩🇪 GERMAN VISITORS | 11 NOVEMBER 2026

A Surprise at Stormwater Lookout!

German geocachers visiting Queensland discovered our Stormwater Lookout geocache in Hervey Bay.

Geocacher gerd9999 left a note and photographed one of our colourful Kevin Bacon Challenge travel bugs.

"Travel Bug OZQCEZ aus der Dose entnommen"

Translated from German: "Travel Bug OZQCEZ taken out of the cache."

Michelle had messaged the visitors, hoping they might help the little traveller continue its journey.

Could this be the beginning of a journey to Germany? We hope so!

The geocache log confirms the reported pickup. The trackable's onward journey remains unconfirmed.
