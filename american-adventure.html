The road to Kevin Bacon continues

Little traveller, big connections

In November 2026, geocacher Beastee welcomed The Kevin Bacon Challenge #5 – The Music Connection to Augusta, Georgia. Before long, the tag was exploring neighbouring South Carolina and visiting a Georgia geocache while its carrier searched for interesting connections to the entertainment world.

This is what the challenge is about: not simply travelling from cache to cache, but learning about the places and people along the way — and sharing that story with someone on the other side of the world.

Making the connections

Where music, television and geocaching meet

🎵 A musical discovery

In a 12 November log at ARMY II, Beastee notes a local link to musicians Josh Kelley and Charles Kelley in Evans, Georgia. A small piece of local music history becomes part of Kevin's journey.

🎬 A television connection

The same log mentions actress Gilland Jones and her role as Jenny in Wizards of Waverly Place . These are discoveries along the challenge's road — not yet a documented six-step link to Kevin Bacon himself.

Thank you, Beastee! You have helped Traveller #5 begin an American chapter full of stories, pictures and unexpected connections. Every new place is another opportunity to explore, learn and bring people together. ❤️
