Pages from the travelling scrapbook

One small tag. So many stories.

On 29 October 2026, ms_sammii retrieved “Kevin Bacon” from Dolphintouch’s Stormwater Lookout! (GCBXB5Z) geocache in Hervey Bay. She planned to take him caching around Maryborough, Childers and Woodgate. By 31 October, he was already making memories around Maryborough — with a little help from ian-and-penny, who logged discovering him during their cache outing together.
