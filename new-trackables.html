Handmade with a little love

These new trackable tags started out as designs printed and laminated at home. They may be small, but their mission is bigger than any one destination: share a story, make a connection and keep moving.

Among the new designs is The Peace & Love Traveller, celebrating love, peace and kindness. Foxes, dolphins and the world of movies are part of the creative spirit of this second wave too.

The hope is that every person who discovers a tag will add another chapter, a photo or a small act of kindness. Maybe one day one will even reach Kevin Bacon!

Tracking note: The six cards above represent the new set, separate from the original six. The public reference number shown is the one confirmed so far; the remaining links and exact card-to-tag names will be added once matched. Live locations and movements are recorded on Geocaching.com, not on this page.
