Scotland — Love, Locks and a Little Adventure

26 November 2026 · Gretna Green

Kevin joined a geocaching maintenance run to Gretna Green, famous for its runaway weddings. No weddings to witness today, but plenty of adventures: love locks, the marriage anvil, countryside and even a Scottish gingerbread man!

Building connections, one stone at a time

Kevin also visited the Auld Acquaintance Cairn beside the River Sark, on the England–Scotland border. Built by people from across the United Kingdom, it welcomes contributions from people of all nations. Every stone, like every story and friendship in our challenge, contributes to something larger.

Thank you to Kevin’s travelling companion for sharing these photographs.
